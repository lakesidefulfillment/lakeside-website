07FAQStraight answers

Questions, answered.

Something we didn’t cover? Call (737) 343-2348 or email inboundleads@lakesidefulfillment.com.

What are your same-day shipping cutoffs?

Monday to Friday, USPS orders in by 2 PM CT and UPS orders in by 6 PM CT ship the same day. Which cutoff applies depends on the carrier your order ships with. Same-day orders are picked, packed, labeled and handed to the carrier from our warehouse in Austin, Texas.

What happens to orders placed after the cutoff?

They ship the next business day, with one exception: on weekdays, UPS orders placed between 2 PM and 6 PM CT still ship the same day. USPS orders placed after 2 PM CT, and any order placed after 6 PM CT, ship the next business day.

Do you ship on weekends?

No. We ship Monday to Friday. Orders placed on Saturday or Sunday ship the next business day.

Where do you ship from, and how long does delivery take?

We ship from our warehouse in Austin, Texas, a central U.S. location. Delivery takes 3–5 days across the U.S., so both coasts are within reach.

What kinds of products do you ship?

We’re a DTC-specialized 3PL for many categories: health and wellness, including supplements and vitamins; beauty and personal care; food and beverage; apparel and accessories; pets; home goods; sports, toys and games; and books and media. We offer product labeling, kitting and bundles, expiration-date (FEFO) rotation, and refrigerated storage for products that need it.

Can I see my inventory and orders in real time?

Yes. Your client dashboard shows inventory, orders and shipments live, with stock levels and reorder notifications.

How is pricing set, and how fast can we start?

Pricing is custom and based on your volume. Tell us what you sell and roughly how many orders you ship a month, and we’ll send a custom quote within 24 hours, with no commitment. Onboarding can take as little as 2 weeks.

What services do you offer besides DTC fulfillment?

B2B and wholesale (retail-compliant packaging, routing guides, palletization), returns processing with inspection and restock, subscription boxes and kitting, and freight and receiving: inbound receiving, cross-dock and freight coordination. Custom packaging and branded inserts are available too.
